BRIGHTMOOR SOFTWARE — FINANCE
Q3 2026 management report, finance facts only.

Annual recurring revenue reached EUR 38.4 million, up 9.1 percent on Q2, and the operating loss narrowed to EUR 1,870,000.

Annual recurring revenue (EUR m): Q2 35.2, Q3 38.4, target 37.5. Gross margin: Q2 71.8 percent, Q3 73.4 percent, target 72.0 percent. Net revenue retention: Q2 112 percent, Q3 114 percent, target 115 percent.

Cloud hosting cost for Q3 was EUR 412,300, which is EUR 38,000 below budget.

Revenue for the quarter was EUR 9,310,000 against a budget of EUR 9,050,000. Gross margin improved to 73.4 percent because of lower hosting cost per customer.

Net cash burn was EUR 2,240,000 for the quarter. Cash at the end of September was EUR 21,650,000, which gives a runway of 29 months at the current burn rate.

Days sales outstanding rose from 41 to 52 days. Two invoices from Medrava Health GmbH, INV-24417 and INV-24452, totalling EUR 188,900, are more than 60 days overdue.

The finance team has completed the data room for the planned Series B round. Corvane Ventures has issued a non-binding term sheet for EUR 30 million at a pre-money valuation of EUR 165 million, with exclusivity requested until 30 November.

Finance is reviewing the commission on the Tessaro deal.

Dinesh Wickramasinghe confirmed that the hiring can be funded from the EUR 38,000 hosting underspend and the Q4 contingency of EUR 150,000.

The board is asked to approve the Q4 operating expense budget of EUR 9,800,000.
