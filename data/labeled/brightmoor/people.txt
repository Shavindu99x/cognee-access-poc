BRIGHTMOOR SOFTWARE — PEOPLE
Q3 2026 management report, people facts only.

Headcount at quarter end: Q2 286, Q3 311, target 315.

The Colombo engineering hub now has 23 engineers. Senior backend engineer Tobias Krämer resigned on 26 September. His notice period ends on 31 December and a counter-offer was declined.

Account executive Nuwan Bandara has raised a formal grievance about the commission paid on the Tessaro deal. The commission statement is CS-3381.

Sofia Brandt joins Brightmoor as VP Sales on 1 November. Her package includes a base salary of EUR 185,000 and 0.4 percent equity, vesting over four years.

Headcount at 30 September was 311, up from 286 at the end of June. Voluntary attrition over the last twelve months is 11.2 percent, against a target of 10 percent.

The employee engagement survey returned an eNPS of +24, down from +31 in March. The lowest scores came from the support team in Berlin.

The annual compensation review will apply a 4.5 percent merit budget from 1 January 2027. Salary bands for engineering roles will be raised by an average of 6 percent. The board is asked to approve the 2027 merit budget.

One employee in Customer Success, Imesha Fonseka, has been placed on a performance improvement plan until 15 December.

The executive team is evaluating a restructuring of the Berlin support team, which could affect up to 14 roles. This must not be communicated to employees before the board decision.

Chathuri Senanayake will open both site reliability roles at band E5 with a salary range of EUR 78,000 to EUR 92,000.

Lukas Hoffmann will be on parental leave from 1 December to 28 February, and the deputy CISO will cover his duties.

Mareike Lorenz intends to step down as CEO at the end of 2027.
