BRIGHTMOOR SOFTWARE — PRODUCT AND ENGINEERING
Q3 2026 management report, product and engineering facts only.

Brightmoor Flow 5.2 was released on 18 August. It introduced the workflow designer, audit trails for admin actions and a 35 percent faster sync engine.

Platform uptime for the quarter was 99.95 percent. The longest outage lasted 47 minutes on 2 September and was caused by a failed database failover in the Frankfurt region. The post-mortem is filed as PM-0912.

Engineering moved 60 percent of batch workloads to spot instances during the quarter.

The Q4 roadmap prioritises single sign-on for all plans, the public API v3 (internal code name KESTREL) and offline mode for the mobile app.

Jonas Feldmann reported that the multi-region failover project needs two additional site reliability engineers.
