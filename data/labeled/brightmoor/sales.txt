BRIGHTMOOR SOFTWARE — SALES, MARKETING AND CUSTOMER SUCCESS
Q3 2026 management report, commercial facts only.

Brightmoor passed 400 customers. New customers: Q2 31, Q3 44, target 40.

New bookings in Q3 were EUR 4,960,000 in annual contract value from 44 new customers. The largest deal was a three-year contract with Tessaro Insurance AG at EUR 610,000 per year, closed on 29 September after a nine-month sales cycle.

Marketing spent EUR 286,000 on paid campaigns and generated 1,940 qualified leads, a cost of EUR 147 per lead.

Three accounts are at risk of churning in Q4: Brandvik Energy AS, Kirkwall Retail Group and Medrava Health GmbH, together worth EUR 830,000 in annual recurring revenue. Customer success has assigned an executive sponsor to each account.

The Nordics team exceeded its quota by 18 percent.
