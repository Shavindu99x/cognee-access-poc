BRIGHTMOOR SOFTWARE — SECURITY AND IT
Q3 2026 management report, security and IT facts only.

Open critical vulnerabilities: Q2 3, Q3 1, target 0.

On 14 August an internal API key was committed to a public code repository and was exposed for 3 hours before automated scanning detected it. The key was revoked, logs show no unauthorised use, and the incident is recorded as SEC-INC-0814.

The annual penetration test by an external firm found 1 critical and 4 high findings. The critical finding, an authorisation bypass in the export endpoint (finding PT-26-01), was fixed on 9 September. Two high findings remain open.

The SOC 2 Type II observation period ends on 31 October and the auditor's report is expected in January.

The quarterly access review removed 37 accounts belonging to people who had left the company. Eleven of those accounts still had administrator rights in the billing system.

Lukas Hoffmann presented the findings of the API key incident.
